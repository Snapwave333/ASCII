- Zones: center-left, upper third mountains, bottom band HUD, etc.
- Normalized: x in [0.1–0.3] of width, y in [0.6–0.8] of height.

Density & Readability:
- At 8K: use subtle shading and nuanced layered backgrounds/props.
- At 1080p: emphasize silhouettes, reduce micro-detail, maintain clear motion.
